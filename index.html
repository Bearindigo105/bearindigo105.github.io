01. about me

I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering student at Penn State, where I lead the avionics subsystem on the ASME VTOL team.

Before that I co-founded FTAD, a nonprofit building a 1:10 scale FPV racing competition, and led automation and vision for FRC Team 1640, all the way to the FIRST World Championship.

I was also a three-season varsity wrestler and team captain, a Senior Patrol Leader in my scout troop, and I play rhythm guitar in a rock band.
