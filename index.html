01. about me

I'm a senior at Great Valley High School. Most of my time goes to FRC Team 1640, where I work on robot software: autonomous paths, turret math and vision.

I'm also CTO of Formula Theory & Automotive Design and captain of the GVHS wrestling team. I like problems where physics, code and a soldering iron all meet.

Outside of that I build things like turning a toaster into a reflow oven, visualising SPAD distance sensors, and writing physics engines for fun.
