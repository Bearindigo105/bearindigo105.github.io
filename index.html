The engine

Every ball on this page is a Particle from TypescriptPhysics, a 2D engine I wrote from scratch. Gravity is a Force, collisions are elastic with an energy loss, and each room's edges are its walls.

Your cursor pushes them, and clicking empty space drops a new one. Each room is its own box with its own floor, and your screen is a camera over the whole grid: scrolling moves the camera, not the balls, so they slide past exactly like the text does.
